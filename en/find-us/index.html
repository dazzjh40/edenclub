Centro Comercial Nilo, Playa del Inglés

The centre is also signposted as Nilo / Salamanca. If you see Salamanca on the signs, you are in the right place.

These three words take you to the entrance of Centro Comercial Nilo. Once inside, we are on the ground floor, first club on the right. The reference cannot be confused with anywhere else in the world, so if you are in a taxi, showing the driver the what3words map is quicker than explaining.

Walking from the Cita, 10 minutes

Most of the Playa del Inglés scene is in the basement of CC Cita, and many visitors assume that is all there is. We are a 10 minute walk away.

Head for the bridge and cross it, past Jardin del Sol. Keep going and you will reach Centro Comercial Nilo. We are on the ground floor, first club on the right. The RIU and Servatur Wikki hotels are the landmarks to aim for.

It is a flat, direct walk and it is free.

If you would rather take a taxi

Ask for Centro Comercial Nilo. Be aware that taxis usually go the long way round, via the main road, because the direct route is not a driving route. It takes longer and costs more than the walk is worth. If you can walk it, walk it.

Walking back late

The route runs alongside a main road, so it is lit and there is traffic at most hours. It is not a dark short cut. It can still feel exposed at two in the morning, particularly on your own, and feeling uneasy is not the same as being at risk. Playa del Inglés is a low crime area and trouble involving visitors is rare.

Walking back in a pair or a group is perfectly pleasant. If you are on your own, or you would simply rather not, ask at the bar and we will call you a taxi.

Opening times

Open 21:00 to 03:00. Last entry 01:30 for everyone.

Single men are considered until 23:30. There is no guaranteed entry at any time: club capacity on the night comes first, as does how you present yourself at the door.

The Canary Islands are on the same clock as the UK, Ireland and Portugal, one hour behind mainland Spain, Germany, the Netherlands and most of Europe. If you are coming from central Europe, 21:00 here is one hour later than the clock you arrived with.
